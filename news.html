Trilateral Summit: Trump, Xi, Putin in Shenzhen

In a historic first, US President Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen for three-way talks on the sidelines of APEC. They signed a joint "Declaration on Peace," focusing on energy flows and nuclear arms limits, though skeptics remain.

The International Court of Justice in The Hague has issued a landmark judgment in the case of The Gambia v. Myanmar, ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority during the 2017 "clearance operations."
