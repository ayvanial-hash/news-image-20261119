One 1024×1024 image summing up the world's news of **Thursday 19 November 2026**:
the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen and its Declaration on
Peace, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the
Dow closing below 50,000 on Anthropic's $81.3B IPO selloff, Cyclone Akvan bearing
down on Odisha, drone strikes on Kyiv including the Dutch embassy, and oil flat
on Hormuz disruptions.

`node main.ts` copies the rendered front page (`entry.png`) to `/output/entry.png`.
No network is needed; two runs write identical bytes.
